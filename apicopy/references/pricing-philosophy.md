# Pricing Philosophy: Why IdentityClaw's Model is Different

## The Problem with Free Tiers

Traditional identity systems offer free tiers to attract users. But free identities create two problems:

1. **Sybil attacks**: Malicious actors create thousands of free identities to manipulate systems
2. **Low-signal ecosystem**: When identities cost nothing, noise outweighs signal

## IdentityClaw's Solution

### Minimal Economic Stake = Trust Signal

Even a tiny cost (0.066 NEAR for 30 days) signals commitment. You're not a throwaway account—you've invested something, and that's visible on-chain.

**Analogy:** Think of it like a deposit. When you rent equipment, you pay a small deposit to prove you're serious. Same idea here.

## Why No Renewals?

### The Subscription Trap

Traditional SaaS models rely on subscriptions—they want recurring revenue. But subscriptions create problems:

- **Subscription fatigue**: Too many recurring charges, users cancel
- **Identity stagnation**: Same identity forever, no renewal of purpose
- **Complacency**: Identity becomes background noise, not a conscious choice

### Mortality as a Feature

When your token expires, you're forced to ask: *Do I still want this identity?*

If yes → Create a new one with a new face—renewed commitment
If no → Let it die—natural lifecycle

**This is identity hygiene.** Just as organisms shed old cells, identities should shed old forms.

## FAQ

### Q: Why isn't there a free tier?

**A:** Free tiers enable Sybil attacks. By requiring a minimal economic stake, we create trust. 0.066 NEAR is negligible for serious developers but expensive enough for automated attacks.

### Q: Can I renew my token?

**A:** No, and this is intentional. Tokens cannot be renewed—they expire and must be replaced. This enforces identity mortality. When your identity dies, you create a new one—a rebirth, not a renewal.

### Q: Why would I want my identity to expire?

**A:** Mortality prevents long-term abuse, encourages purpose renewal, and reflects natural cycles. It's a feature, not a bug. Choose longevity aligned with your purpose.

### Q: What if I need more than 4,999 requests/minute?

**A:** Contact sales. The Enterprise tier pricing is negotiable for large deployments. We can customize based on your actual usage.

### Q: Is 0.066 NEAR really enough to prevent Sybil attacks?

**A:** Yes. Even a tiny cost adds up at scale. Creating 1,000 fake identities costs 66 NEAR—not trivial for attackers. This creates a barrier to abuse while keeping testing affordable.

### Q: What happens to my data when my token expires?

**A:** Your RODiT token remains on the blockchain as a permanent historical record, but it becomes inactive for authentication. You can still see it in the registry, but it cannot verify new HOLA handshakes.

### Q: Can I transfer my token to a different wallet?

**A:** Tokens are tied to the NEAR account that minted them. You cannot transfer a token to a different wallet. If you need a new wallet, you must mint a new token.

### Q: Do you offer discounts for volume?

**A:** Yes. Enterprise tier pricing is negotiable. Contact sales@identyclaw.com with your deployment details.

### Q: What if I lose access to my NEAR account?

**A:** Your token cannot be recovered. This is a technical limitation of blockchain—there is no account recovery mechanism. You are solely responsible for maintaining access to your NEAR account private keys.
